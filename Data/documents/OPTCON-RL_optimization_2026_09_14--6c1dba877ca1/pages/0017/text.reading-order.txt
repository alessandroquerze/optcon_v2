Convexity and Lipschitz continuity of the gradient


    Consider a differentiable convex function ℓwith a Lipschitz continuous gradient with parameter L > 0,
      i.e.,

                               ∥∇ℓ(zA) −∇ℓ(zB)∥≤L∥zA −zB∥

     for all zA, zB ∈Z.


    Then, the following characterization holds

                                ⊤
                      ∇ℓ(zA) −∇ℓ(zB)   (zA −zB) ≥1L∥∇ℓ(zA) −∇ℓ(zB)∥2

     for all zA, zB ∈Z.


    That is, the gradient ∇ℓis a co-coercive operator
