Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) necessary conditions

    Let z⋆be a regular local minimum of

                              min ℓ(z)
                                     z∈Rd
                                       subj.to gj(z) ≤0     j ∈{1, . . . , r}
                                           hi(z) = 0      i ∈{1, . . . , m}

    where ℓ, gj and hi are C1.
    Then ∃unique µ⋆j and λ⋆i , called Lagrange multipliers, s.t.

                              ∇1L(z⋆, µ⋆, λ⋆) = 0

                                         µ⋆j ≥0
                                    µ⋆j gj(z⋆) = 0     j ∈{1, . . . , r}
