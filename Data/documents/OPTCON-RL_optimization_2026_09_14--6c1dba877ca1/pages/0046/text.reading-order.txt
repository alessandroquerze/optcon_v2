Example: Gradient Method applied to Quadratic Programs


    Quadratic program

                                           1
                                   min 2zT Qz + qT z                                          z∈Rd
    with Q = QT ≥0 ∈Rd×d and q ∈Rd.


    Steepest descent method

                                      zk+1 = zk −γk∇ℓ(zk)

     for a Quadratic Program is given by

                                     zk+1 = zk −γk(Qzk + q).
