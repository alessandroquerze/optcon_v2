Necessary conditions of optimality (unconstrainted)


     First order necessary condition (FNC) of optimality (unconstrained)
    Let z⋆be an unconstrained local minimum of ℓ: Rd →R and assume that ℓis continuously differentiable
    (C1) in B(z⋆, ϵ) for some ϵ > 0. Then ∇ℓ(z⋆) = 0.

    Second order necessary condition of optimality (unconstrained)
       If additionally ℓis twice continuously differentiable (C2) in B(z⋆, ϵ), then ∇2ℓ(z⋆) ≥0 (the Hessian
    ∇2ℓ(z⋆) of ℓis positive semidefinite).


    Remark Points ¯z satisfying ∇ℓ(¯z) = 0 are called stationary points. They include minima, maxima and
     saddle points.
