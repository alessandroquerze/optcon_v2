Warning on notation in Optimization and Optimal Control


    Notation z
   We denote z ∈Rd the generic optimization variable of a generic optimization problem, e.g.

                                       min ℓ(z),
                                               z∈Rd
    where ℓ: Rd →R cost function.





    Notation zk
   We denote zk ∈Rd the solution estimate at iteration k ∈N of an optimization algorithm for the
     optimization problem.
