Active constraints and regular points





    Set of active inequality constraints
    For a point z, the set of active inequality constraints at z is A(z) = {j ∈{1, . . . , r} | gj(z) = 0}.


    Regular point
   A point z is regular if the vectors ∇hi(z), i ∈{1, . . . , m}, and ∇gj(z), j ∈A(z), are linearly indepen-
     dent.
