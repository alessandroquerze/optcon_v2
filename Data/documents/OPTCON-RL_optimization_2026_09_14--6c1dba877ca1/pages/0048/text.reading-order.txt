Optimization over convex sets


    Consider the optimization problem

                                       min ℓ(z),
                                           z∈Z
    where Z ⊂Rd is nonempty, convex and closed, and ℓis continuously differentiable on Z.
    Remark Recall that a set Z is closed if it contains all of its limit points.



                                     Important
                              The function ℓcan be nonconvex.



    Optimality Conditions
       If a point z∗∈Z is a local minimum of ℓ(z) over Z, then

                                    ∇ℓ(z∗)⊤(¯z −z∗) ≥0     ∀¯z ∈Z
