Convex functions

    Convex function
    Let Z ⊂Rd be a convex set. A function ℓ: Z →R is convex if for any two points zA and zB in Z and
     for all θ ∈[0, 1], then

                             ℓ(θzA + (1 −θ)zB) ≤θℓ(zA) + (1 −θ)ℓ(zB).





    Remark A function ℓis concave if −ℓis convex. A function ℓis strictly convex if the inequality holds
      strictly for zA̸ = zB and θ ∈(0, 1).
