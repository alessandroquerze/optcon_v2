Example: Projected Gradient Method for Quadratic Programs


    Quadratic program

                                           1
                                   min 2zT Qz + qT z                                       z∈Z
    with Q = QT ≥0 ∈Rd×d and q ∈Rd.


    Projected gradient method

                                    zk+1 = PZ zk −γk∇ℓ(zk)

      is given by

                                  zk+1 = PZ zk −γk(Qzk + q)  .
