Newton’s Method via Sequential Quadratic Programming (SQP)


     Start from a tentative solution z0
    For k = 0, 1, . . . (up to convergence)
        1. Compute ∇ℓ(zk), Bk, ∇h(zk)
        2. Obtain (∆zk, ∆λkQP) from

                          ∆zk = argmin ∇ℓ(zk)⊤∆z + 12∆z⊤Bk∆z
                                      ∆z
                                               subj.to h(zk) + ∇h(zk)⊤∆z = 0.                         (2)

          with ∆λkQP the Lagrange multiplier associated to the optimal solution of (2).

        3. Update

                                           zk+1 = zk + ∆zk
                                            λk+1 = ∆λkQP
