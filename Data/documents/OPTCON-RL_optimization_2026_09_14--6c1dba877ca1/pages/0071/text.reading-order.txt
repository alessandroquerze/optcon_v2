Newton’s method for equality constrained problems

   KKT points can be found by solving a root finding problem in variables z, λ with r(z, λ) = ∇L(z, λ).


    Newton’s method for this root finding problem reads as

                                      zk+1     zk    ∆zk
                            =   +
                                     λk+1     λk    ∆λk

    with ∆zk, ∆λk solution of

                                  ∆z
                               ∇2L(zk, λk)    = −∇L(zk, λk)
                               ∆λ

    where

                       Bk     ∇h(zk)                      ∇ℓ(zk) + ∇h(zk)λk
           ∇2L(zk, λk) :=                         ∇L(zk, λk) :=
                        ∇h(zk)⊤     0                                 h(zk)

    and Bk := ∇211L(zk, λk).
