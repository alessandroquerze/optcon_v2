Strong convexity and gradient monotonicity
A function ℓis strongly convex with parameter µ > 0 if for zA̸ = zB and θ ∈(0, 1)
ℓ(θzA + (1 −θ)zB) < θℓ(zA) + (1 −θ)ℓ(zB) −µθ(1 −θ)∥zA −zB∥2
The gradient of a differentiable strongly convex function satisfies
