Second order sufficient condition of optimality (unconstrained)
Let ℓ: Rd →R be twice continuously differentiable (C2) in B(z⋆, ϵ) for some ϵ > 0. Suppose that
z⋆∈Rd satisfies
∇ℓ(z⋆) = 0
and
∇2ℓ(z⋆) > 0 (positive definite).
Then z⋆is a strict (unconstrained) local minimum of ℓ.
