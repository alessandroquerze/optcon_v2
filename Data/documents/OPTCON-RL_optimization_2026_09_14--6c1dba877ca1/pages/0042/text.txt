Gradient methods: convergence result (Armijo)
Proposition (convergence with Armijo step-size)
Let {zk} be a sequence generated by a gradient method zk+1 = zk−γkDk∇ℓ(zk) with d1I ≤Dk ≤d2I,
d1 > 0, d2 > 0. Assume that γk is chosen by the Armijo rule and ℓ(z) be continuously differentiable.
Then, every limit point ¯z of the sequence {zk} is a stationary point, i.e., ∇ℓ(¯z) = 0.
Remark Recall that a vector z ∈Rd is a limit point of a sequence {zk} in Rd if there exists a subsequence
of {zk} that converges to z.
