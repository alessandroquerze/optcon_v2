Gradient methods: convergence result (constant/diminishing)


     Proposition (convergence with constant or diminishing step-size)
    Let {zk} be a sequence generated by a gradient method zk+1 = zk−γkDk∇ℓ(zk) with d1I ≤Dk ≤d2I,
    d1 > 0, d2 > 0. Assume that for some L > 0

                           ∥∇ℓ(z) −∇ℓ(y)∥≤L∥z −y∥,     ∀z, y ∈Rd.

    Assume either
        1. γk = γ > 0 sufficiently small, or
        2. γk →0 and P∞t=0 γk = ∞.
    Then, every limit point ¯z of the sequence {zk} is a stationary point, i.e., ∇ℓ(¯z) = 0.
