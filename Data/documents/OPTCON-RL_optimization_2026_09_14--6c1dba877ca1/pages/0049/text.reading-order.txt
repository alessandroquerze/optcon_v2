Optimization over convex sets


     Projection over a convex set
    Given a point z ∈Rd and a closed convex set Z, it can be shown that

                                   PZ(z) := arg min ∥y −z∥2
                                               y∈Z
     exists and is unique.

    The point PZ(z) is called the projection of z on Z.
