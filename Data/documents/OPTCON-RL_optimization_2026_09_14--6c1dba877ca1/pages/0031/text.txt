Gradient methods: selecting the descent direction
Several gradient methods can be written as
zk+1 = zk −γkDk∇ℓ(zk)
k = 1, 2, . . .
where Dk ∈Rd×d is a symmetric positive definite matrix.
It can be immediately seen that
−∇ℓ(zk)T Dk∇ℓ(zk) < 0,
i.e., dk = −Dk∇ℓ(zk) is a descent direction.
