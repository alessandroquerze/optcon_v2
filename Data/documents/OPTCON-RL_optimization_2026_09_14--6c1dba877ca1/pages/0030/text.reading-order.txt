Gradient methods: introduction

    The update rule

                                      zk+1 = zk −γk∇ℓ(zk),

    can be generalized to so called gradient methods

                                        zk+1 = zk + γkdk,

    with dk such that

                                        ∇ℓ(zk)T dk < 0.
