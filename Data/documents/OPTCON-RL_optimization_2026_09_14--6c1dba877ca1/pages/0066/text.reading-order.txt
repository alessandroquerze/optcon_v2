Quadratic Programming (constrained)


    Let us consider quadratic optimization problems with linear equality constraints.

    Quadratic program

                                             1
                                    min 2zT Qz + qT z                                           z∈Rd
                                               subj.to Az = b

    with Q = QT ∈Rd×d, q ∈Rd, A ∈Rm×d and b ∈Rm.
