Notation

    Optimal solution
   We denote ℓ(z∗) the optimal (minimum) value of a generic optimization problem, i.e.

                                              ℓ(z∗) = min ℓ(z),
                                                   z∈Rd
    where z∗is the minimum point (optimal value for the optimization variable), i.e.,

                             z∗= argmin ℓ(z),
                                                  z∈Rd
