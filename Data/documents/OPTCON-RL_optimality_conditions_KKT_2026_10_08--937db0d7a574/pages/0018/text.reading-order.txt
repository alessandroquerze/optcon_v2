Discrete-time optimal control problem (constrained)


   We look for a solution of the discrete-time optimal control problem



                                           T −1
                           min   X ℓt(xt, ut) + ℓT (xT )                              (5a)
                         x0∈Rn,x∈RnT ,u∈RmT  t=0
                                          subj.to xt+1 = ft(xt, ut),   t = 0, . . . , T −1
                                               r(x0, xT ) = 0,                                      (5b)
                                                  gt(xt, ut) ≤0,   t = 0, . . . , T −1

    where
      •  ℓt : Rn × Rm →R is the so-called stage cost,
      • ℓT  : Rn →R is the terminal cost,
      • r : Rn × Rn →Rp0 identifies a boundary constraint on initial and final states,
      •  gt : Rn × Rm →Rp for each t identifies point-wise constraints on state and input at some time t.
