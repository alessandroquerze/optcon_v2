System linearization and tangent hyperplane
That is, T(¯x,¯u)T = {(∆x, ∆u) ∈RnT × RmT | ∇1h(x, u)⊤∆x + ∇2h(x, u)⊤∆u = 0} is the set of
trajectories (∆x, ∆u) of
∆xt+1 = At∆xt + Bt∆ut,
with
At = ∇1ft(¯xt, ¯ut)⊤
Bt = ∇2ft(¯xt, ¯ut)⊤.
Remark Recall that for a vector function f : Rn+m →Rn, ∇f is the transpose of the Jacobian matrix.
