Unconstrained optimal control problem (discrete-time)


   We look for a solution of the following discrete-time optimal control problem

                                       T −1
                           min X ℓt(xt, ut) + ℓT (xT )                                  (4a)
                           x∈Rn,u∈Rm
                                           t=0
                                      subj.to xt+1 = ft(xt, ut),   t ∈0, . . . , T −1                     (4b)

    with given initial condition x0 = x0 ∈Rn, where ℓt : Rn × Rm →R is the so-called stage cost while
    ℓT  : Rn →R is the terminal cost.


    Remark An optimal control problem constrained only through the dynamics (without boundary and path
     constraints) is named unconstrained optimal control. This notation will be clarified in the following.
