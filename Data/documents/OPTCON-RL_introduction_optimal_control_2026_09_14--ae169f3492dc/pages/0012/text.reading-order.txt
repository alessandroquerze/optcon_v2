Linearization of a system about a trajectory


    Given the dynamics

                                     xt+1 = ft(xt, ut)      t ∈N0,

    and a trajectory (¯x, ¯u) (i.e., ¯xt+1 = ft(¯xt, ¯ut) for all t ∈N0), the linearization of (1) about (¯x, ¯u) is
     given by the linear (possibly) time-varying system

                            ∆xt+1 = At∆xt + Bt∆ut      t ∈N0,

    with At and Bt the Jacobians of ft, with respect to state and input respectively, evaluated at (¯x, ¯u).
