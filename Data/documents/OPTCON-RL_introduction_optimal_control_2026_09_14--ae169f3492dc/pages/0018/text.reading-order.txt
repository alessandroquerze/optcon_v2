Optimization problem (minimization)


   We can write our optimization problem as

                               min  ℓ(z)
                                      z∈Rd
                                        subj.to hi(z) = 0      i = 1, . . . , m
                                             gj(z) ≤0     j = 1, . . . , r

    where hi : Rd →R and gj : Rd →R.


   We can write it more compactly as

                                      min  ℓ(z)
                                              z∈Rd
                                                 subj.to h(z) = 0

                                                    g(z) ≤0

    where h : Rd →Rm and g : Rd →Rr.
