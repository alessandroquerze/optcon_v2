Discrete-time optimal control problem


   A discrete-time optimal control problem can be written as

                                       T −1
                          min X ℓt(xt, ut) + ℓT (xT )
                                       x0,x1,...,xT
                                       u0,...,uT −1  t=0

                                     subj.to xt+1 = ft(xt, ut),   t = 0, . . . , T −1
                                          r(x0, xT ) = 0,
                                             gt(xt, ut) ≤0,   t = 0, . . . , T −1

    where ℓt : Rn × Rm →R is the so-called stage cost while ℓT  : Rn →R is the terminal cost.
